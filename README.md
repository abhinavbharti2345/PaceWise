# PaceWise 💰

> A modern student-focused budget tracker designed to help you make your monthly money last.

PaceWise helps students manage their monthly allowance, track daily spending,
keep up with bills, and stay on top of money owed between friends.

Instead of simply showing how much money you've spent, PaceWise focuses on
**how much you can afford to spend today** and automatically adjusts your
daily budget based on your spending habits.

---

## ✨ Features

### 📊 Smart Daily Budget

Set your monthly allowance and PaceWise calculates your daily spending limit.
